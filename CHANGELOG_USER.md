# What's New

What each version of EDSSI brought, as EDSSI shows it after an update. Plain words for
commanders; the technical record is CHANGELOG.md.

## 1.2.0

- **Plot routes on Spansh from the Route tab.** Galaxy, neutron and fleet carrier routes for any
  of your ships, with stops on the way. Follow the route, or save it for later.
- **Start with the game.** Turn it on under Settings, Interface, and EDSSI opens whenever Elite
  Dangerous starts, leaving the game in focus.
- **ChatGPT sign-in shows straight away** when you pick your ChatGPT plan, before you save.
- **Copying a system from the route map** works again.

## 1.1.0

- **A stronger default model** for the assistant.
- **Whisper listens by default**, on your PC, so hearing you needs no OpenAI key.
- **Clearer Quickstart**, with neater settings.

## 1.0.0

- **First release.** EDSSI follows your journal while you play and shows the session at a glance:
  where you are, where you're heading, your fuel, and what your unsold data is worth.
- **Mission tab.** The route ahead, the bodies worth a probe, and the life in each system, with the
  species likely to grow there and what each could pay.
- **An assistant you can talk to.** Ask about your trip, your ship or the galaxy by voice or text.
  It searches Spansh, plots routes, reads the web, and answers aloud.
- **Routes from Spansh.** Load a route's CSV and EDSSI puts the next system on the clipboard after
  every jump, with a 3D map of the whole trip.
- **Materials, commodities and ships.** Engineering to-do lists and the trades that cover them,
  shopping lists with market searches and buying plans, and every ship you own slot by slot, ready
  to open in EDSY or Coriolis.
- **Warnings in flight.** Low fuel, unscoopable stars ahead, high gravity, carriers nearby, and
  your own carrier about to jump.
- **Uploads.** Sends your journal to EDSM, Inara and EDDN, so you don't need another tool running
  for that.
- **Keeps itself up to date.** New versions download in the background and install the next time
  EDSSI starts.
